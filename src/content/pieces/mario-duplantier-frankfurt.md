description: "Anyone that was in the stadium that day and time knows the sun and the venue had a show of their own. Deutsche Bank Park is a covered venue that has a row of open space right where the ceiling starts. Before Gojira went up stage the sun came through that row of open space, and instead of blinding everyone the light hit a large amount of fake smoke making a beautiful, hazy scene. I have a huge pile of photos with that light that lasted practically the entire Gojira show, and from that pile I wanted to showcase this moment in particular: Mario seems to be engulfed in the haziness, like becoming one. Photo is very cropped to give an intimate feeling, portraying the man more than the drummer. Original color is a pale yellow however that didn't match the energy of this show, cause despite the haze the band was full power mode blasting their instruments with peak technique."
